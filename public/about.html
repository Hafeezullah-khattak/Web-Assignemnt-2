About This Portal

The University Lost & Found Portal helps students and staff report lost items, list found items, and reconnect belongings with their rightful owners. This project was built as Assignment 02 for the Web Technologies course (CS 311 / CS 224), Department of Computer Science, UET Peshawar.

Rules

  • Only report items that genuinely belong to you or were actually found by you.
  • Provide accurate descriptions and locations to help with identification.
  • Once an item is claimed, please update its status to "Returned".
  • Misuse of the portal (false reports) may result in account restrictions.
